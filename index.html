About the salon

An intimate retreat on the Upper East Side.

Acqua Salon is more than just a place to get your hair done — it's a retreat. Our intimate, modern space was designed to make every client feel pampered from the moment they walk through our doors.

With years of expertise and a passion for artistry, our talented stylists deliver personalized looks that enhance your natural beauty. We use only premium professional products, including Morocanoil and other luxury brands, to ensure your hair looks and feels its absolute best.

  • Walk-ins welcome — appointments recommended on weekends
  • Precision cuts, blowouts, color, and highlights
  • Keratin treatments and deep conditioning repair
  • Bridal styling and special occasion updos
